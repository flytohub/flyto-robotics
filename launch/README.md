# ROS 2 and Gazebo launch files

Launch files here are development, simulation, and external-adapter integration
surfaces. They are not a requirement to provision a production robot with
Flyto2 software.

A physical TurtleBot3 may use upstream TurtleBot3, Nav2, SLAM Toolbox, sensor,
and camera launch files directly. Flyto2-specific launch files retained here
exist for lab regression and historical compatibility and must not become a
second production authority path.
