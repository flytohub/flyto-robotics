# Serial-bus arm M0: state, joint limits, measured stop (simulated)

Owner: claude
Branch: claude/am-arm200-m0
Date: 2026-10-18

## What changed

- `flyto_robotics/arm_bus.py` (new): `ArmBus` protocol (positions in radians
  by joint name, torque state, goal write, torque enable/disable; failures are
  `BusError`) and `SimulatedArmBus` with fault knobs: `stalled`,
  `drop_after_writes`, `torque_off_ignored`, `sag_rad`, `missing_joints`,
  `connected`.
- `flyto_robotics/arm_adapter.py` (new): `ArmProfile` / `parse_profile`
  (`flyto.arm-profile.v1`, closed key sets, nothing defaulted) and
  `SerialArmAdapter` serving `arm.state`, `arm.move_joints`, `arm.halt`
  through `adapter_contract.CallRequest` / `CallResult`.
- `flyto_robotics/arm_contracts.py` (new): `capability_rows(profile)`, the
  `flyto.capability-contract.v1` rows built from the same profile.
- `flyto_robotics/lerobot_arm_bus.py` (new): `ArmBus` over an injected
  LeRobot-style motors bus. Unverified; imports nothing, opens no port.
- `examples/arm-profiles/am-arm200-follower.json` (new): placeholder profile,
  `limits_accepted: false`.
- `tests/test_arm_adapter.py`, `tests/test_arm_contracts.py` (new).
- `docs/ARM_M0.md` (new, linked from README), CHANGELOG Unreleased, STATE,
  ROADMAP, tasks.

## Why

flyto-cloud ROADMAP "CONVERGED P0" step 3: the AM-ARM200 follower arm is the
second technology, added as an adapter and capability contract only. M0 is
state, joint limits and a proven stop. The hardware has not arrived, so this
prepares the adapter and the tests that the real arm is checked against.

Decisions: a target outside a limit is refused, never clamped; moves are
refused until the profile's limits are accepted on the physical arm; the
example's stop is `hold` because torque-off can drop a loaded arm; a lost
connection mid-move is `state_known: false`, never re-sent, and blocks moves
until a state read; an unconfirmed stop blocks moves until a confirmed stop.
Rejected: a vendor register map in the adapter, and pack rows in
flyto-modules-robotics before the limits are measured.
